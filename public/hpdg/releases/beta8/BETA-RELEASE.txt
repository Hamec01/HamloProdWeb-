HPDG 0.1.0 Beta 8 — HamloProd
Windows 10/11, x64, VST3 only.

Includes the VST3 plugin, the full library of 1147 audio samples, the guide
and the rights notices. No activation and no internet connection are needed
to use the plugin; your license key (sent by email after the purchase) is only for
in-plugin updates. The Microsoft Visual C++ Runtime is included and installed
if needed.

Close your DAW before installing. The plugin goes into the standard Windows
folder: C:\Program Files\Common Files\VST3\HPDG.vst3.
The samples live inside that bundle, next to the plugin binary.
After installing, rescan VST3 plugins in your DAW.

This is a beta: keep backups of your projects and report bugs with your DAW,
Windows version, genre and the steps that led to the problem.
Standalone is not part of this release.

What's new in Beta 8 (since Beta 7):
- Boom Bap Underground (formerly Russian Underground): two-bar loop drums,
  four groove families with their own swing, kick shapes, ghost snares and
  hats, and its own bass line built on the kicks. The bass no longer hits
  just before a kick. A new HamloProd underground kit (18 one-shots) is
  used only by this substyle. Projects saved with Beta 7 still open.
- Sample influence 1 / 2 / 3 in the Instrument Rack header: how strongly
  a loaded sample steers Guide generation. 3 (default) works as in Beta 7,
  2 follows the sample more loosely, 1 keeps only the lightest guidance.
  Copy Break is not affected.
- Copy + Sample Tempo: copy a break at the sample's own tempo (detected or
  typed). The pattern length equals the selection (1-16 bars), and the
  sample and the copied MIDI loop together. The DAW tempo is not changed.
- Sample tempo detection lands on a whole BPM when the loop length
  confirms it (77 instead of 76.90, 88 instead of 87.97).
- Loops are read as whole bars: a 4-bar loop is no longer read as 3 bars.
- Export Full MIDI writes one track laid out like a drum map: Kick on
  note 36, Snare 37, HiHat 38, Open Hat 39 and so on, with the bass below.
  Dragging a single lane works as before.
- Your own samples: load WAV, AIFF, FLAC, MP3 or OGG files (up to 30 s,
  several at once) or drop them onto a lane row. A loaded sample stays on
  its lane when you regenerate.
- Boom Bap Classic with a sample: the bass follows what "Play w/ HPDG"
  actually plays (Trim, DAW Sync, loop restart), and Bass 1 answers in
  the gaps of the sample's low end.
- LuxuryTrap: rebuilt hi-hat carrier and rattles, two-bar kick call and
  answer, 808 kept in its register, rebalanced lane volumes.
- DarkTrap: a new drum kit of 131 sounds with 20 808s, a different 808 on
  every Generate (a locked 808 stays). The 808 rhythm is closer to the
  reference beats, with triplet pickups, a smoother sidechain and glides
  on the target note. Guide accents land where the sample is heard.
- Copy Break: snare flams stay on the Snare lane, and quiet kicks before a
  snare stay kicks.
- The +Lane button and the RG hint were removed from the rack header.

What's new in Beta 7 (since Beta 6):
- Rebuilt LuxuryTrap kick/808 phrases and hat rolls; 79 Luxury kit assets.
- HPDG 808 Play/Stop audition with repeating notes.
- Six new factory 808 voices: ATL 1-3 and Dark 1-3 (998 audio samples total).
- Distinct ATLClassic and DarkTrap kick, hat and 808 rules; compact bass
  register and improved sample-guided bass/kick behaviour.
- Trap kicks avoid the snare and its immediate aftermath.
- Trap Rolls: Off / Style / Fills; finer grid and triplet editing.
- Sub808 uses the HPDG 808 synth engine, with bass strip, sound editor,
  sample layer, filters, drive and presets. Drive level compensation and
  inline preset saving; Boom Bap bass enabled by default.
- Sample Analysis infers the key from Basic Pitch notes, with chroma
  fallback. Manual key selection retains priority.
- Reduced Sub808 note-end crackle and UI-related audio interruptions.

What's new in Beta 6:
- The installer, the guide (FAQ) and the release notes are in English.
- FAQ: where to find your license key and how HPDG uses it.

What's new in Beta 5:
- Update button next to the version in the header. Click it to check for
  a new version: Latest, Update: <version> or Offline.
- In-plugin updates for buyers: enter your license key once (from your
  purchase email) and HPDG downloads the new installer, checks that it is
  complete and unchanged, and starts it when you confirm.
- Click "HPDG" in the header for the menu: Update, Instruction, Site.
- No internet? HPDG works exactly as before.

What's new in Beta 3:
- New genre Atmospheric: Pulse, Two-Step and Broken rhythms, a melodic bass
  over minor harmony and its own drums and basses (64 new samples).
- Intensity 1-5 in the Instrument Rack header: 1 is the simplest beat, 3 is
  as before, 5 is the most hits. The next Generate / Mutate / RG use it.
- Strength knob on every lane (instead of pan): left removes the weakest
  hits, right adds hits typical for the genre.
- Trap: 808 in the right register, bass lines across the whole phrase with
  glides, kick and snare after reference trap packs; the hat Strength knob
  removes whole rolls first and keeps the quarter notes.
- Sub808 plays the HPDG synth sub by default (4 variants, tuned to C2).
- Dragging MIDI / WAV into the DAW: the plugin window hides once the file
  leaves it and comes back after the drop (FL Studio and others).
- Sample Analysis: Guide generation is now the default mode.

What's new in Beta 2:
- Trim sample: Loop / Play / Stop buttons; dragging the edges no longer
  restarts the sound; a click on the waveform sets the play cursor.
- Trim sample: "Selection = N bars" field - choose how many bars the
  selection holds and the tempo follows (for example exactly 4 bars).
- Sub808 plays notes as written; the - / + buttons on the bass lane change
  the octave (Shift - a semitone), and MIDI export keeps it.
- Dark and Steel skins, switched right from the SKIN menu.
- Techno: a second drum set (tech house) and basses, 100 new samples.
